# Product Context

## Product

Neura-Shadow is a bilingual engineering portfolio and technical publishing surface for Lu Tsung-Hsin. It presents repository-backed systems work, research, and carefully bounded prototypes while supporting a Nuxt 3 content experience and an optional Supabase-backed CMS.

## Audience

- Engineering recruiters and hiring teams evaluating systems breadth and evidence quality.
- Technical collaborators assessing backend, robotics, Edge AI, UAV, and computer-vision work.
- Readers looking for clear architecture notes and implementation boundaries.

## Brand Register

The product uses a brand register: warm, precise, mechanical, and evidence-grounded. The visual identity is premium but restrained, with ivory and beige surfaces, refined teal, muted graphite, and limited amber accents. Content should feel authored by an engineer, not generated from a generic portfolio template.

## Product Personality

- Calm confidence without seniority inflation.
- Systems thinking expressed through useful relationships and architecture.
- Honest status language that separates project-applied work, research use, prototypes, working knowledge, and current focus.
- Readable bilingual content with Traditional Chinese as a first-class locale.

## Anti-References

- Black cyberpunk dashboards or dense particle fields.
- Generic AI-template grids, glass everywhere, gradient text, and decorative technical diagrams.
- Tiny badges, overcrowded cards, or skill lists without evidence context.
- Inflated seniority labels, production deployment, fixed performance, publication-status upgrades, or real hardware validation without direct evidence.

## Accessibility and Interaction

- Preserve readable contrast, comfortable body text, visible focus states, and keyboard-operable controls.
- Keep Hero text and calls to action above decorative canvas layers.
- Respect reduced motion and provide a meaningful static fallback when WebGL is unavailable.
- Reduce Three.js complexity on mobile, pause while hidden, and dispose resources on route unmount.

## Current Positioning

Primary positioning: Embedded Linux & Distributed Real-Time Systems Developer.

Traditional Chinese positioning: Embedded Linux 與分散式即時系統開發者.

Supporting capability line: High-Concurrency Go, Cloud-Native Systems, Jetson Edge AI, ROS 2, UAV Integration, and Nuxt.

The homepage is intentionally compressed: one summary sentence, two compact and visually distinct research or experience callouts, and four core-capability groups. Detailed technical inventory, evidence status, architecture, ROS 2 responsibilities, QoS, and profiling belong on the About page.

Implementation-oriented NSTC applied R&D covers architecture development and system integration within assigned scope across heterogeneous UAV, USV, and UGV platforms. Independent image-processing and computer-vision research submitted to IEEE Transactions on Multimedia is a separate body of work and must never be presented as an NSTC outcome or deliverable.

Embedded Linux, NVIDIA Jetson, ONNX, TensorRT, GStreamer, V4L2, and advanced ROS 2 capabilities must remain labeled as current focus, working knowledge, or planned extension unless repository evidence supports a stronger status.
